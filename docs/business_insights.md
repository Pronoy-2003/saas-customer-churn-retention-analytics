# Business Insights

## Business Problem

The SaaS business is losing customers and needs to understand **why customers churn, when churn occurs, which plans and acquisition channels are most affected, and how much revenue is at risk**.

The analysis uses customer, subscription, monthly activity, and payment data to identify churn and retention patterns and help the business make data-driven customer retention and revenue decisions.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Customer Churn

- Total customers: **10K**
- Churned customers: **1.43K**
- Overall churn rate: **14.29%**
- Retention rate: **85.71%**

The business has a significant churn base, making customer retention an important revenue and growth priority.

### 2. Churn by Subscription Plan

- **Starter:** 5.70% churn rate
- **Professional:** 4.65%
- **Business:** 3.07%
- **Enterprise:** 0.87%

The **Starter plan has the highest churn rate**, while Enterprise customers show the lowest churn.

### 3. Churn by Acquisition Channel

- **Paid Social:** 19.24%
- **Paid Search:** 17.76%
- **Email Marketing:** 13.67%
- **Organic Search:** 13.32%
- **Sales:** 13.05%
- **Partner:** 11.67%
- **Referral:** 10.61%

**Paid Social and Paid Search** have the highest churn rates, while **Referral** has the lowest.

### 4. Customer Retention Trend

Retention declines throughout the year:

- January: **99.50%**
- June: **98.90%**
- September: **98.93%**
- December: **97.25%**

The downward trend indicates that retention becomes weaker over time and should be monitored by customer tenure and cohort.

### 5. Revenue Performance

- Total revenue: **$13.78M**
- Churned revenue: **$133.72K**
- Revenue at risk: **$1.60M**
- Average lifetime revenue: **$1.38K**

Revenue at risk is substantially larger than currently churned revenue, highlighting the importance of proactive retention.

### 6. Revenue at Risk by Plan

- Business: **$0.55M**
- Professional: **$0.44M**
- Enterprise: **$0.42M**
- Starter: **$0.20M**

The **Business plan has the highest revenue at risk**, followed by Professional and Enterprise.

### 7. Revenue Contribution by Plan

- Enterprise: **$4.8M**
- Business: **$4.5M**
- Professional: **$3.3M**
- Starter: **$1.1M**

Enterprise and Business customers contribute the largest share of revenue.

## Business Recommendations

1. Focus retention efforts on **Starter customers**, where churn is highest.
2. Review **Paid Social and Paid Search** acquisition quality because these channels have the highest churn.
3. Prioritize high-value customers with significant **revenue at risk**.
4. Monitor customer retention across tenure and cohorts to identify when retention begins to decline.
5. Use customer activity, payment failures, and support interactions as supporting indicators when investigating churn.
